# ADR-008: Initial Distribution Policy

Status: accepted

## Decision

Start with manual operator review and distribute each qualified project to no more than two relevant providers.

## Why

It protects early consumer experience, lets the operator learn provider quality, and avoids indiscriminate lead resale before unit economics are known.

## Review trigger

Revisit after sufficient evidence on provider response, consumer satisfaction, quote rates, and close attribution. Amend this ADR rather than silently changing policy.
