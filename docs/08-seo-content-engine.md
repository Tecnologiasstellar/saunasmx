# SEO and Content Engine

## Content families

- transactional service pages;
- city and territory pages;
- pricing/cost pages;
- comparison pages;
- educational buying guides;
- provider profiles and galleries;
- calculators and readiness checklists;
- FAQs and glossary terms.

## Page eligibility

A programmatic page may publish only when it has enough unique value. Initial gate:

```text
active provider count >= 2
serviceability confirmed
local or category-specific information present
unique FAQ or evidence present
canonical URL assigned
factual review complete
```

Do not mass-generate thin city/category pages.

## Required page fields

```text
marketplace
page_type
slug
search_intent
target_query
title
description
canonical_url
indexing_policy
structured_data_type
content_owner
sources
last_reviewed_at
conversion_action
```

## Technical SEO requirements

- server-rendered public content;
- canonical URLs and alias redirects;
- generated sitemap per marketplace or domain;
- robots rules per environment;
- noindex for previews, drafts, internal portals, and thin pages;
- Open Graph/Twitter metadata;
- structured data only when it accurately represents visible content;
- fast mobile layout and accessible forms;
- internal links between guides, category pages, provider pages, and questionnaire CTA.

## AI content controls

AI drafts must retain source citations, confidence, reviewer, and review date. Do not publish unreviewed health, legal, price, safety, or regulatory claims. Provider-submitted claims must be labeled as provider-provided until verified.

## Acquisition loop

```text
Search/paid/social traffic → useful content → questionnaire CTA
→ qualified project → anonymized aggregate insight → better content and matching
```
